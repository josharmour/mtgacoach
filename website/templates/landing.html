The essentials stay visible. Open the plan, board, forecast assumptions or activity when you want more detail.

Know whose decision it is

The current decision separates your response from the opponent's priority. See whether you're playing, Autoplay is acting, or the board is being reassessed.

Compare the paths from here

A green line marks the leading supported path. Expand an alternative to inspect its actions and projected life totals. Inspecting a path doesn't change Autoplay's choice.

A moving balance, not win odds

The outlook tilts as the board assessment changes. It is a heuristic read of the position, not a percentage chance of winning. A leading path can still be a projected loss.

When the board changes, Coach withdraws the old forecast while it reassesses. Future draws and unknown opponent spells are outside the modeled paths.

Questions

Before your first match.

Does coaching need the bridge?

Spoken coaching can read Arena's Player.log with Detailed Logs enabled. Autoplay also needs the platform bridge to submit actions. Setup & Repair checks the connection.

Are the outlook and paths win predictions?

The outlook is a heuristic board assessment, not win odds. Paths show modeled continuations under stated assumptions, not future draws or unknown opponent spells. Open Forecast assumptions in Coach for the limits; a green leading line is not a promise of winning.

Where do the model and voices run?

Coaching and generated commentary use our hosted model service. Chatterbox speech is hosted too; Kokoro speech runs locally. You can choose the speech provider in Speech & voices.

What leaves my computer?

Model requests send the game context needed for coaching or commentary to our service. Hosted Chatterbox also receives the text to speak. Local Kokoro speech stays on your computer.

By default, the app shares redacted finished-match records to help improve Coach. Use Tools → Share Match Logs to Improve the Coach to turn sharing off.

What happens when the trial ends?

Continue with a Patreon subscription and link it to get your license key. The trial does not automatically charge you. Existing trial machines do not get a new trial by reinstalling.

Is this official?

No. mtgacoach is unofficial fan software, not made or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.
