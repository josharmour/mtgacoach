## 1 · The climb

**Subject**  Back to Mythic in 11 days

Mono-Red Burn won my October 27 promotion game and took me from Diamond 1 to Constructed Mythic. On November 11, the same deck did it again. I used MTGA Coach on both climbs. The first took about 27 days from the season reset. The second took about 11.

Those were busy days. By the second promotion, Arena had counted 745 ranked matches on that account. My other account reached Mythic by November 9, and the biggest difference I saw there came from switching decks.

If you’re climbing this season, which deck are you taking into the queue? Hit reply with the list. I’d love to see what you’re playing.

## 2 · The deck switch

**Subject**  Re: Back to Mythic in 11 days

Here’s the deck comparison I mentioned. On my second account, The Notary Hobbits went 97–131 in the ranked Brawl games I could match to the list. Minsc & Boo went 91–73 through my first confirmed Mythic screenshot. Same queue, different stretches of the climb, and a move from 42.5% to 55.5% wins.

It’s worth asking two questions after a close loss. Was there a better line with the cards I drew? And if I played that matchup ten more times, does my list have enough cards that answer the opponent’s plan? Coach can walk through the first question during a match. The second is a good reason to open the deck builder.

If you’re choosing your next Standard deck, open Tools → Decks → Build a stronger deck. Compare a list against the cards you own and check what it costs in wildcards before you craft it.

Josh

## 3 · Limited and Discord

**Subject**  Re: Back to Mythic in 11 days

One of my favorite Coach recommendations this month was a common. In pack three of a Bloomburrow draft, my black-green deck needed a two-drop and Food for forage. Bakersbane Duo gave me both. That explanation made the pick click.

In the November 14 Sealed Qualifier Play-In, the decision I remember was a cut. Coach compared builds and came back with red-white, 23 spells and 17 lands. Leaving a blue-red legend in the sideboard kept the mana simple enough to play creatures and removal on time.

I’m considering an MTGA Coach Discord where we could look at decisions like those together. Would you bring a decklist that’s stalled, a screenshot of two possible draft picks, or a Sealed pool with one cut left? Reply with yours. I’d use those examples to decide what to open first.

Josh
