# Drafting, gameplay reliability, and deck visibility — October 26, 2026

This update collects the draft audit repairs, gameplay execution fixes, diagnostic improvements, and new limited-deck preview. It also publishes the two existing local auto-quest commits. Source changes and regression coverage are not a claim that every historical incident is resolved in live play.

## New features

### Limited-deck comparison window

The desktop now receives the proposed limited deck before the engine writes it to Arena. A nonmodal window displays the recommendation beside up to two distinct alternatives, with card quantities, mana costs, basic lands, printed mana curve, and cards left out. Existing build plans and cut explanations are displayed when available. Duplicate copies in the pool are counted correctly.

The current preview can be reopened through **Tools → Draft deck recommendation**. It is read-only: autoplay continues with its recommendation. There are no alternative-selection buttons or card-art thumbnails in this version. Scores from Draftsmith are not copied or presented as our own, and alternatives are not claimed to produce better win rates. Preview failures do not block deck submission.

### Explain draft rating trade-offs

When the selected card has a lower supplied 17Lands quality band than an available alternative, the advisor identifies a strongest higher-band alternative and preserves the comparison in narration. The prompt requests a concrete need in the actual pool and acknowledges the power being sacrificed. Missing comparisons are labeled unverified instead of receiving an invented explanation.

This compares the quality bands actually supplied to the coach. It does not ingest live Untapped scores or detect every numeric gap within a band. A lower-rated card may still be a reasonable choice for a deck's curve, interaction, or mana needs.

### Reports after manual recovery

Autopilot bug reports now retain bounded semantic Arena requests, submitted responses, bridge failures, and rejection context. A report filed after recovering a stuck prompt can still preserve the original request and response identity. Match/game boundaries and ID namespaces are kept distinct; unknown input origin is not attributed to the user or autopilot without evidence.

These are semantic game inputs, not a raw mouse/keyboard recording. The trace is included in local diagnostic reports; this update does not upload the local bug ledger or play logs.

### Local draft audit utility

`scripts/audit_untapped_drafts.py` compares saved coach recommendations with locally recorded Companion draft picks and scores. Missing scores, forced picks, attribution uncertainty, and separate draft formats are preserved. The utility does not call Untapped APIs. Agreement with another evaluator is not a win-rate estimate or proof of optimal drafting.

### Auto-quest work included in this push

The existing local commits add bounded quest progression using owned decks, then simplify it to one automatic mastery-XP mode. See `docs/auto-quest.md` for its controls and constraints. Publishing these commits does not enable the mode in the running session.

## Draft correctness fixes

- Preserve Cube identity instead of inferring a retail set from individual card printings and loading that set's primer and statistics.
- Keep the first four opening picks free of persisted lane, curve, engine, and splash pressure. Scope synergy links to owned cards for ordinary single-pick packs, while retaining appropriate multiple-pick behavior.
- Synchronize persisted plans with accepted picks. A vetoed recommendation, invalid answer, or incomplete multi-pick answer must not become a card the next plan assumes was drafted.
- Separate artifact presence, artifact-token creation, sacrifice, and actual artifact-entry triggers. Preserve conditional exile and sacrifice cautions.
- Recognize supported Equipment tutor/deployment, Living Weapon and For Mirrodin bodies, own-graveyard spell recursion, and Exhaust relationships without inventing unsupported activations.
- Supply local card metadata to no-primer fallback ranking instead of effectively selecting by pack order.
- Give narrowly defined free, unconditional reusable artifact mana an explicit unrated prior. Conditional, sacrifice, imprint, tapped-entry, and paid activations do not receive that prior; measured ratings keep precedence.

## Gameplay and execution repairs

### Request handling and recovery

- Yield to fresh state when a plan expires instead of coaching or handing off from a stale request. Remove stale post-cast snapshot fallback.
- Improve bounded recovery after repeatedly cancelled combat planning and preserve current-window identity during repairs.
- Use exact creature instance identities for ambiguous duplicate attackers/blockers and retain final request validation.
- Distinguish sequential damage-assignment requests using fresh request evidence rather than treating identical labels as the same prompt.
- Preserve decision evidence binding and avoid treating plan expiry alone as proof of manual intervention.
- Skip replay commands unsupported by the native adapter while retaining supported bridge behavior and genuine errors.
- Distinguish a completed null bridge result from malformed transport data during match startup.

### Selections and card identity

- Handle card-name choices in Arena's card-title namespace, including Anointed Peacekeeper-style prompts, revealed/public candidates, static subsets, and submission-time identity checks.
- Carry ability-specific trigger context through selection prompts rather than substituting the parent permanent's unrelated rules.
- Include revealed library selection candidates and preserve relevant zone information.
- Preserve legal optional choices and context-dependent graveyard selections instead of treating a heuristic highlight as a prohibition.

### Mana and targeting

- Improve X-budget and payment-capacity reasoning for fixed multi-mana sources, alternative abilities, colored requirements, and paid filters without double-counting a permanent.
- Exclude fetch lands without their own mana ability from immediately available mana.
- Count the supported Notary/Halfling variable-output case using current controlled permanents; broader unknown or conditional mana remains uncertain.
- Keep Channel-in-hand abilities separate from battlefield abilities and avoid treating a Channel-specific discount as a global spell discount.
- Preserve valid mixed beneficial/damage targeting, including supported Charm modes, while retaining controller and harmful-benefit checks.
- Distinguish immediate blink from lasting removal and retain friendly ETB, token, ownership/control, and combat-specific effects.

### Combat and rules facts

- Account for chosen-player protection in targeting and damage forecasts.
- Correct lifelink, trample, double-strike, and protection arithmetic, including negative-power edge cases.
- Correct summoning-sickness annotations: a fresh creature can crew; restrictions apply to attacking and its own tap/untap-symbol abilities.
- Refresh rules data/cache when relevant source facts change and tighten unsupported combat/rationale claims.

### Diagnostics and reload readiness

Once a match bundle has been saved locally, HTTP upload retries are tracked separately from the file-writing worker. Upload throttling should no longer masquerade as an unfinished local save when evaluating reload readiness.
